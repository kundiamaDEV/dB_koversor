--- Formulas apresentadas ao utilizador ---------------------------
conversao                   apresentado     valor interno       verificacao
dBm -> W                    mW = 10^(dBm / 10)  |  W = mW / 1000
W -> dBm                    mW = W × 1000  |  dBm = 10 × log10(mW)
dBW -> dBm                  mW = 10^((dBW + 30) / 10)  |  dBm = 10 × log10(mW)
dBm -> dBV                  mW = 10^(dBm / 10)  |  V = √(P × R)  |  dBV = 20 × log10(V)
dBV -> W                    V = 10^(dBV / 20)  |  P = V² / R  |  W = mW / 1000
dBu -> dBV                  V = 0,775 × 10^(dBu / 20)  |  dBV = 20 × log10(V)
